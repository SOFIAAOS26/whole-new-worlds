# SANDS OF TIME

### [INTRO — intimate]

If we're only stardust...

If we're just a grain of sand  
in the vastness of the universe...

If we're only a breath  
inside the endlessness of time...

If we're just a fleeting spark  
among the stars above...

God, what a lucky man I am  
to have found you.

### [VERSE 3 — CHOOSING YOU]

I want you for this lifetime  
and for eternity.

**I want to stay inside your heart,  
every single day.**

and see it shining there.

**You're the place

### [BRIDGE — YOUR UNIVERSE]

Let's expand our universe.

I've got so many dimensions  
I wanna show you,

and so many emotions  
that you awakened in me.

Let's go to the moon  
and sit here together.

Let's invent  
something no one's ever known.

**The universe seemed so vast,  
but tonight it fits entirely  
between your hands.**

I wanna kiss the stars upon your skin,  
travel through your constellations.

Let me make you feel

Let me build for you  
a corridor of stars.

And if you want to admire it,

Let's walk together  
through planets and comets.
